FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Local small businesses in the Sacramento area: restaurants, cafés, bakeries, salons, barbers, trades and home services, plus event organizers and pop-ups.

Do you use AI? Is the content fake?

We use AI-assisted tools to draft captions, transcribe audio, and speed up editing. A person reviews every piece. We never invent reviews or testimonials, and we never pass off AI images as photos of your food, work, or space.

Do I have to approve everything?

Yes. Nothing goes on your accounts without your written approval.

Is there a long-term contract?

No long-term lock-in. We'll go over the terms with you before you sign up.

Who owns the photos and videos?

Our intent is that you can use the content you've paid for on your own channels. Usage terms are spelled out in writing before we start.

Do you post for me, or just hand over files?

Either one. We can schedule approved posts on your accounts or deliver ready-to-post files.

What areas do you cover?

The Sacramento area. Ask us about locations farther out.

Do you run paid ads?

Our packages cover content creation and organic posting. Ad spend isn't included.
